DATA COLLECTION NOTICE

This game records anonymous gameplay data for an educational
data-analysis portfolio project.

Recorded information may include:
- randomly generated game identifier
- enemy name and word length
- text entered during each attempt
- accuracy
- time taken
- success or failure
- player health
- encounter and attempt numbers

The game is not intended to collect names, email addresses, locations,
or other personal information.

By continuing to play, you agree that anonymous gameplay records may
be stored and analysed for this project.
